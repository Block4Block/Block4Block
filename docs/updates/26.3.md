# Minecraft 26.2 → 26.3

The fourth and last step to 26.3, one version at a time (Server Queue SQ2 and SQ45). Started 27 November 2026, right
after 26.2 went live (SQ66) and its pull requests were merged (SQ67). Paper 26.3 build 263 (stable).

## What 26.3 adds
Found by comparing the Paper API's block, item and mob lists for 26.2 and 26.3, then reading minecraft.wiki:
- **Dappled forest**, a new biome, with the **poplar** tree: a full wood family (logs, wood, planks, stairs, slab,
  fence and gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate, signs, hanging signs, shelf, boats) and leaves in three
  autumn colors (red, orange, yellow; each tree picks one).
- **Red shrub**: a small plant in dappled forest patches; bone meal grows another next to it.
- **Shelf mushroom**: grows on poplar logs and sticks to the side of any full block; bone meal makes a small one large
  (a large one drops two); it bounces what lands on it, softening the fall; it goes in stews.
- **Straw bed**: a one-night bed crafted from hay bales. Sleeping skips the night and clears the weather, but doesn't
  set your spawn; it falls apart with no drop when you wake; it doesn't explode in the Nether or the End.
- **Slabs and stairs for every wool and concrete color** (64 blocks).
- **Cushions** (16 colors): entities, not blocks, made from wool slabs. You sit on them; any hit breaks one, and it
  drops itself.
- **Abandoned camps** with straw beds and cushions, and **explorer maps** to camps and buried structures.
- No new mob. Data format 121; protocol 777; Java 25 (as 26.2).
- Nothing removed from the API.

## Changes
### Block4Block 4.15 (branch `update/26.3`)
- Built against the Paper 26.3 API (build 263) with JDK 25.
- Following a rule the config already has (no owner choice needed):
  - poplar's forms go in every list pale oak's same form is in: logs and the three leaves exempt; planks, wood,
    stripped wood, slabs, stairs, fences, doors, trapdoors, buttons, plates, signs and the shelf cost one and drop
    nothing; stripped poplar wood can be broken with poplar wood, as every stripped wood can;
  - the wool slabs and stairs cost one and drop nothing, like wool; the concrete ones also, and are Free in Claims, like
    concrete;
  - the potted poplar sapling costs one and drops nothing, like the other potted saplings.
- The owner's choices:
  - the poplar sapling is exempt, like the other saplings; the pale oak sapling stays as it is (SQ68);
  - the red shrub costs one and drops nothing, like flowers (SQ69);
  - the shelf mushroom is a utility block: Free in Claims, and costs one and drops nothing elsewhere, like the Golden
    Dandelion (SQ70);
  - the straw bed is exempt, and TNT drops it, like beds (SQ71).
- Cushions are entities, so Block4Block doesn't touch them, as with paintings.

### Essential datapack (branch `update/26.3`)
- `pack.mcmeta` supports data format up to 121.
- **`overlay_121`, every loot table in 26.3's form.** 26.3 rewrote how loot tables are written, and it quietly
  ignores the old keys: the pack's tables loaded with no error, but lost every condition and function. On the copy,
  killing axolotls with a command dropped plain player heads: no texture, no name, and no player needed. The changes,
  found by diffing vanilla 26.2's tables with 26.3's: `conditions: [...]` is one `condition` (several become `all_of`)
  and a condition's `condition: X` is `type: X`; `functions` is `modifier` and a function's `function: X` is `type: X`;
  `filtered`'s `modifier` is `on_pass`; `block_state_property {block, properties}` is `match_block {blocks, state}`;
  a count given as `{min, max}` needs `type: minecraft:uniform`; damage tags and tag entries are written with `#`.
  Without the overlay, the datapack failed to load at all (the creeper's tag entry), and the server refused to start.
  `tools/datapack/conv263.py` does the conversion and checks itself against vanilla: it turns 1,036 of vanilla 26.2's
  tables into exactly what 26.3 ships, and the rest differ only where vanilla changed its own content.
  `tools/datapack/overlay121.py` writes all 181 tables the pack uses into the overlay. Older versions keep their files.

## Bots on 26.3, without ViaVersion (SQ63, SQ65)
The released bot libraries stop at 26.1, and PrismarineJS's own `pc_26_3` branches are unfinished: minecraft-data's
26.3 has a new protocol file but still points its blocks, items and entities at 26.1's lists, and that protocol leaves
out several 26.3 changes. `tools/test-server/bots/mineflayer-26.3.py`, run after `mineflayer-26.2.py`, fills the gaps
from 26.3 itself:
- the block, item, entity and sound lists are built from the game's own reports (its data generator, which Paper's jar
  carries); every one of the 35,723 block states checked against the report decodes to the right block and properties;
- the packet layouts were read from the 26.3 server jar's codecs (Mojang's names): four new packets numbered as the
  game's packet report has them; the arm swing replaced by `punch`; the teleport confirmation now carries the position;
  light masks sent as BitSet bytes; entity movement sent as a path; item components renumbered (122 types), with the new
  attack and interact animations, waxed and cushion color, and the pot decorations' new layout; a recipe's tag slot now
  a set of items; advancements' positions moved;
- mineflayer ends each tick, which 26.3 now requires (a second position packet in one tick gets the client kicked).

## Checked on a copy (Paper 26.3 build 263, Java 25, port 25577)
A copy of the 26.2 copy, world included.
- All 16 plugins enable; the datapack loads with no errors and no warnings.
- Every `nbt` condition in the datapack (53) matches a mob summoned with that data.
- Mob heads: killed without a player, axolotls, sheep, cows and foxes dropped none in 20 kills each. Killed by a player,

- Bots, all on 26.3: `tooltipbot.js` 6/6, `pluginsbot.js` 8/8, `lecternbot.js` 4/4, `disguisebot.js` 3/3,
  `claimbarbot.js` 6/6, `breakbot.js` 29/29. New in the break checks: poplar logs, leaves and sapling and the straw bed
  are free to break; poplar planks, the wool stairs, the concrete slab, the red shrub and the shelf mushroom cost one and
  drop nothing outside a claim, and the red shrub is refused without one; in your own claim the concrete slab and the
  shelf mushroom are free and drop themselves. 56 checks.

## Other ways to break the new blocks (tested on the copy)
Block4Block charges for blocks a player breaks, and decides what TNT-broken blocks drop; every other way of breaking
follows vanilla. Tested with `indirect263.py`, reading drops from the server:
- **Red shrub, shelf mushroom and poplar sapling** drop themselves, free, when the block under or behind them is broken,
  when water flows onto them, when a piston pushes them, and when a creeper explodes next to them; TNT drops nothing.
  This is how flowers and the Golden Dandelion behave too.
- **Straw bed**: water, pistons, creepers and TNT all drop it (it's exempt and on the TNT list, like beds).
- **Wool and concrete slabs and stairs, poplar planks**: pistons move them without breaking; water leaves them; TNT and
  creepers dropped none in these tests.
- Lava left nothing to pick up in any test.
